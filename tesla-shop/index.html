    How to play

    1. Go to the Front Office and talk to Kim, Ryan, or Jordan Sham. They name the repair type upfront (brake service, water leak, etc.).
    2. Take the ticket to the Customer Lot (west), get the vehicle, and bring it into the right bay (east wall).
    3. Talk to the right tech. Wrong specialty = insults + redirect.
    4. If parts are needed, the tech radios Moe — wait at the bay for delivery (or visit Parts for flavour).
    5. Finish the repair, survive the backhanded praise, pull the next job. Clear all five to end the shift.
